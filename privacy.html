Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

ForgeFrame Studio ("we") operates an internal publishing tool ("the App") that uploads videos created by ForgeFrame Studio to YouTube channels owned and operated by ForgeFrame Studio.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by ForgeFrame Studio to manage its own channels. It is not offered to the public, and no outside users sign in to it.

2. Google user data we access

With the channel owner's permission, the App uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and visibility;
  • read basic information about our own channel (such as the channel name and upload status).

We do not access any other Google account data.

3. How the data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored securely on our private server and are used only to publish our own videos.
  • We do not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.
  • We do not collect personal information about viewers.

4. Limited Use

ForgeFrame Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, ForgeFrame Studio agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

6. Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked or the App is retired, stored tokens are deleted from our server within 30 days.
